Rule

Background

This is a midnight black-market showdown. Four brokers are eyeing six covered items on the table, each competing alone with no allies. On your turn, you can reveal an item, rotate it to change whom the seller is willing to deal with, or spend your chips to purchase it. An item can only be bought when it's open to you—when the seller is willing to make that deal with you. Each item has its own value and special ability that affects future actions. Whoever reaches seven influence points first wins the night on the black market.

Setup

  • Each player starts with 6 cards, which can be:
    • Black
    • Red
    • Flip/Rotate
  • Place 6 cards face down in the center as the Board.

Each board card is either:

  • an Action Card (worth -2 to 3 points), or
  • a Point Card worth 3, 2, or 1 points.
  • Players sit across from each other.
  • You may only collect cards that face your direction.
  • Each player can have a maximum of 8 cards in their hand.

How a Round Works

1. Roll the Dice

Roll two dice (each 1–6) to determine which board cards you interact with this turn. Each die corresponds to one action.

2. Spend Cards to Perform Actions

You may perform up to two actions this turn (one per die rolled).

For each action on the selected board card, you spend Black / Red / Flip cards as follows:

A. Flip a face-down card

Cost: One Flip card or Two cards of same color

B. Rotate a face-up card

Cost: One Flip card or Two cards of same color

C. Take a face-up card facing you

Cost: 3 cards matching the card's required color

  • If the card shows Black, spend 3 Black
  • If the card shows Red, spend 3 Red
  • If the card shows Black/Red, spend 3 Black or 3 Red

When you take the card, you score its points.

3. Not Enough Cards?

For each action you cannot or choose not to perform, you can:

  • Draw 1 card from the deck
  • If you already have 8 cards, you must discard 1 card first before you draw the card.
  • End that action

Action Cards

Action Cards (worth -2 to 3 points) can include abilities such as:

  • +1 or –1 to future die rolls
  • Rotating an extra board card
  • Taking an additional draw
  • Swapping two board positions
  • Blocking an opponent's next rotate, etc.

Their effect triggers immediately when taken. Each action card ability can only be used once per turn.

End of Game

The first player to reach 10 points wins. (7 points for the presentation)
